## Start here

- **Learn** — Explore quantum programming through the [FatQat documentation](https://fatqat.readthedocs.io).
- **Build** — Use [FatQat](https://github.com/spaceqat/fatqat) for quantum programming, compilation, and simulation.
- **Exchange** — Ask questions and share ideas in [FatQat Discussions](https://github.com/spaceqat/fatqat/discussions).
- **Connect** — Visit our [website](http://space-qat.com/en.html) to discover community activities and collaboration opportunities.
